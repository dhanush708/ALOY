# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who interacts with the ALOY project — regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

---

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior includes:**
- Being respectful and constructive in all communications
- Offering helpful feedback and well-reasoned suggestions
- Acknowledging others' contributions and perspectives
- Focusing on the issue, not the person

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Posting spam, irrelevant content, or deliberately misleading information
- Attempting to impersonate the creator or project maintainers
- Sharing others' private information without consent

---

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:
- A warning
- Removal of the offending content
- Temporary or permanent ban from project interactions
